# Latent Trajectory Supervision vs Auxiliary Waveform Decoding

To avoid penalizing minor harmless phase shifts during waveform generation and to enable robust extrapolation over continuous time, we decided to prioritize primary supervision directly within the continuous latent space (**Latent Trajectory Supervision**). Inspired by MIRA's continuous dynamics extrapolation, the primary loss compares predicted future latent states against future target segments mapped through a target encoder ($L_{latent\_dynamics}$). Raw waveform reconstruction serves as an auxiliary decoder objective utilizing a composite **Phase-Tolerant Waveform Decoding** loss (incorporating local smooth L1, spectral morphology, event timing, and inter-lead consistency penalties) to preserve high-fidelity morphology without requiring exact pointwise phase alignment.
